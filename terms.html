QuickCourt Terms and Conditions

1. Booking Policy

Users must provide correct information while booking a court. A booking is confirmed only after successful submission of the booking form.

2. Payment Policy

Under the Pay Later option, users must pay 50% of the total booking amount in advance. The remaining 50% must be paid at the venue.

3. Cancellation Policy

  • Cancellation 3 or more days before booking: 10% charge
  • Cancellation 2 days before booking: 20% charge
  • Cancellation 1 day before booking: 30% charge
  • Cancellation on the same day: 50% charge

4. Refund Policy

The refundable amount will be calculated after deducting the applicable cancellation charge from the paid amount.

5. User Responsibility

Users must arrive at the venue on time and follow the rules and regulations of the selected court.

6. Agreement

By confirming a booking, the user agrees to follow all QuickCourt Terms and Conditions.
